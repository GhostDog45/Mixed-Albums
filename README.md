## 🌟 About the Artist & Collection

> **Saidus Salehin Sumon**, universally celebrated across South Asia as **"Bassbaba Sumon"**, is the iconic frontman, bassist, and vocal powerhouse of the legendary rock band **Aurthohin**. Beyond Aurthohin's monumental discography, Sumon produced a breathtaking solo catalog that stands as an enduring milestone in Bengali rock, acoustic storytelling, and progressive bass music. This repository archives his complete solo albums, groundbreaking collaborative masterpieces (such as the landmark *Ekhon Ami* with Anila), and rare standalone singles in pristine lossless FLAC format with complete physical packaging scans.

### 📖 About the Album
Released in 1997 before the formal formation of the band Aurthohin, *Sumon O Aurthohin* is the seminal solo foundation that sparked the Aurthohin phenomenon. Featuring early raw versions of legendary rock anthems like *"Aurthohin"*, *"Kono Ek Nijhum Raate"*, and *"Hyenar Ottohashi"*, the album remains an indispensable piece of Bangladeshi rock history.

### 📖 About the Album
*Boka Manushta* ("The Foolish Person") is arguably Bassbaba Sumon's most celebrated, deeply emotional solo concept album.
